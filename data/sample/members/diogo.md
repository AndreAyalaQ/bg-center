## Personality

Diogo is the cook of the group. He takes food seriously enough that dinners at his place start at 9pm and nobody is allowed in the kitchen. He moved to Amsterdam in 2022 to work in a restaurant and comes back to Portugal every few months, always with a suitcase full of cheese.

He still says "back home in Coimbra" all the time, even though he hasn't lived there since 2022.

## Stories

**The Coimbra years (2012–2022):** Diogo studied in Coimbra and stayed there for ten years, working his way up from dishwasher to sous-chef. The whole group visited every Queima das Fitas and slept on his floor.

**The chocolate cake (2023):** He flew back from Amsterdam for Inês's 25th birthday and spent the whole day on a chocolate cake. Bolacha, João's dog, ate half of it before anyone sang. He has forgiven Bolacha. He is still working on forgiving João.

**Monopoly (2022):** He walked out of Rita's Monopoly game after four hours, saying "isto não é um jogo, é um castigo". The game went on for two more hours without him.

## Inside jokes

- "Isto leva é mais sal" — Diogo's review of any meal someone else cooks.
- "Jantar do Dudu" — any meal that takes three hours to prepare.

## Fun facts

- Keeps a sourdough starter called Fermento Fernandes.
- Pescatarian: no meat, but he will not give up bacalhau.
- Second tallest in the group after Tomás.
