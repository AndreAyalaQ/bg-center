## Personality

Tomás is the calm one and, at 1.91 m, by far the tallest. Anything on a high shelf at any party is his job. He collects vinyl records, mostly Portuguese jazz, and will explain each one whether you asked or not.

He has a famously bad sense of direction, which the group considers a safety issue. Never let Tomás navigate.

## Stories

**The Porto night (2019):** Tomás's version of the São João trip: it was entirely Rita's fault. He wanted to take an Uber to Campanhã; Rita said she knew the way. They missed the last train and Joana rescued them at 2am. He says her sofa was the best sleep of his life.

**The bill-splitting app (2023):** After one too many dinners where Rita's maths left someone short, Tomás built a small app to split restaurant bills. The group used it twice, then went back to Rita's mental arithmetic.

## Inside jokes

- "Pipo, can you reach it?" — said at every party, by everyone.
- "Works on my machine" — Tomás's answer when his app gives the wrong total.
